# Document Intelligence & Knowledge Synthesis

This skill empowers JARVIS to read, interpret, analyze, and synthesize insights from diverse document formats across the filesystem.

## Core Capabilities

1. **Scientific Paper & Article Analysis**:
   - Extract abstracts, methodology, findings, and citation networks from LaTeX, Markdown, and text documents.
   - Cross-reference terminology with user knowledge in Obsidian (`search_obsidian`) and Hierarchical Contextual Memory (`search_hierarchical_memory`).

2. **Cross-Format Translation & Restructuring**:
   - Convert raw notes or draft outlines into publication-ready LaTeX or Markdown formats.
   - Extract tables, references, and mathematical formulas for inclusion in Overleaf projects.

3. **Knowledge Consolidation**:
   - Durable technical facts and architectural findings are automatically consolidated into Obsidian memory (`append_obsidian_note` or `store_contextual_memory`).
   - Ensures recurring project details, parameters, and citations remain readily accessible across all future sessions.
